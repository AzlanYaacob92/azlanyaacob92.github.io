Conclusion

How it works: the limiting reactant fixes how much product can form. For aA + bB → cC, the theoretical amount of C is n(limiting) ÷ its coefficient × c. Then percentage yield = actual ÷ theoretical × 100. This tool finds the limiting reactant for you — it does not show that working; for that, use Chemculate Limiting Reactant.
Relative atomic masses follow common data-booklet values (H = 1.0, O = 16.0, Cl = 35.5, Cu = 63.5 …). State symbols and spectator H⁺/OH⁻ are omitted. Molar gas volume: 24.0 dm³ mol⁻¹ at RTP (20 °C, 1 atm), 22.4 dm³ mol⁻¹ at STP (0 °C, 1 atm). Answers are given to 4 significant figures. This tool shows the method; always re-check against your own mark scheme.
